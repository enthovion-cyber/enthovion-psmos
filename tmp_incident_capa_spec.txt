================================================== CORE PURPOSE
==================================================
This tab must answer:
What corrective and preventive actions are required?
Which RCA root cause, causal factor , barrier failure, immediate action, evidence gap, or regulatory
finding generated each action?
Is each action corrective, preventive, interim, permanent, engineering, administrative, training,
procedural, MI, MOC, PSSR, HAZOP/LOPA, or regulatory?
Who owns each action?
What is the due date, priority, status, and risk ranking?
Has the action been implemented?
Is implementation evidence attached?
Has effectiveness been verified?
Are overdue or high-risk actions escalated?
Are all root causes covered by at least one CAPA or justified?
Is CAPA ready for review and closure?

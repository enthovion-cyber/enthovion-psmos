================================================== CORE PURPOSE
==================================================
This tab must answer:
Which PSM, EHS, engineering, maintenance, document, action, and compliance records are
connected to this incident?
Which records were the source of the incident context?
Which records were created because of the incident?
Which records need review, update, revision, action, or closure before the incident can close?
Are linked MOC, PSSR, PTW, MI, HAZOP , LOPA, SDS, SOP , CAPA, equipment, chemical, evidence, RCA,
barrier , and regulatory records current and valid?
Are there missing required links?
Are there stale, superseded, restricted, or broken links?
Is linked-record readiness complete for review and closure?

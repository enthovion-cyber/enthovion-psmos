================================================== CORE PURPOSE
==================================================
This tab must answer:
Is the incident investigation ready for formal review?
Are all required investigation sections complete?
Are there unresolved blockers, missing evidence, open actions, overdue CAPA, missing regulatory
reports, or incomplete RCA?
Who must review and approve the incident?
Which approval workflow applies based on severity, potential severity, PSM/PSE tier , regulatory
reporting, injury, fatality, chemical release, barrier failure, and company/site rules?
Has each reviewer approved, rejected, requested changes, or signed electronically?
What comments, decisions, and signatures exist?
Can the incident be closed?
If not, why exactly?
Is closure supported by audit trail and required approvals?

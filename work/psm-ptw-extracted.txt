1. Permit Types - Required 9 Types
Build all nine permit types with type-specific fields, validation, blocking rules, required approvals, and required certificates/forms.

required. LEL threshold must be site-configurable; default should

controlled site override such as <5% only if allowed by site 
standards.
Cold Work Mechanical maintenance, inspection, and non-intrusive activities 
without ignition risk. Lower risk workflow but still requires area 
authority approval and worksite closeout.
Confined Space Entry Atmospheric testing mandatory. Rescue plan required before entry.

2. Permit Lifecycle
 Statuses: Draft, Submitted, Approved, Issued, Active, Suspended, Extended, Closed, Cancelled.
 Draft: permit is being prepared and is not valid for work.
 Submitted: requester has sent permit for review/approval through Workflow Engine.
 Approved: required approvals are complete but permit is not yet issued/active.
 Issued: permit issuer has issued permit to permit holder/performing authority.
 Active: work is authorized to proceed.
 Suspended: work is paused because of expiry, gas test overdue, conflict, unsafe condition, shift handover suspension, or 
manual suspension.
 Extended: permit validity has been extended with required checks and approvals.
 Closed: work complete, area safe, de-isolation complete where applicable, personnel accounted for, and closure signed.
 Cancelled: permit cancelled before completion with reason.
 Cannot activate until required approvals, isolations, gas tests, workforce acknowledgements, rescue plans, and conflict 
checks are complete.
 Cannot close until worksite closeout, de-isolation, final gas test if required, personnel sign-out, and closure verification are 
complete.
 Every status change creates audit log and permit history event.
3. Equipment Integration
 Equipment Tag selector searches Equipment Registry by tag, name, unit, area, and site.
 Selecting equipment auto-fills equipment name, unit, area, type, status, criticality, safety-critical flag, fluid service, and 
hazard class.
 Critical equipment automatically sets PTW risk to High or requires additional approval per site rules.
 Parent/child equipment relationships appear inside the permit. Selecting pump may show motor, seal, associated isolation 
valves, blinds, and related equipment.
 Isolation points can be pre-populated from Equipment Registry valve/blind/isolation metadata.
 Existing open PTWs, MOCs, actions, inspections, and linked safety records for that equipment must be visible in permit 
creation screen.
 Permit must appear in Equipment Registry Linked Records tab, Equipment Timeline, and Equipment Overview open permit 
count.
4. Isolation Management / LOTO
 LOTO register: each energy source listed with energy type, source description, isolation point, lock number, lock holder, 
applied by, verified by, timestamp, and status.
 Valve isolation list: each valve tag, required position open/closed, normal position, locked state, confirmation checkbox, 
confirmer, and timestamp.
 Spectacle blind/spade register: blind number, location, installed by, confirmed by second person, install timestamp, and 
removal timestamp.
 Multi-energy types: electrical, mechanical, pneumatic, hydraulic, thermal, chemical, gravitational, stored pressure, and other 
configurable energy types.
 Permit cannot activate until all required isolation points are confirmed. This must be a hard system block.
 De-isolation workflow must systematically reverse isolation points before equipment return to service.
 Isolation certificate PDF must be printable for physical posting on equipment during work.
 Every isolation confirmation requires electronic signature or user identity plus timestamp and audit log.

5. Gas Testing
 Gas testing must support O2, LEL, H2S, CO, and custom gases such as SO2, Cl2, NH3, HF, VOC, benzene, or site-
specific gases.
 O2 required range: default 19.5% to 23.5%, site-configurable.
 LEL threshold must be configurable by site, permit type, area, and fluid service. Confined space default may be <10% LEL. 
Hot work default should support stricter 0% LEL policy where required; site override can allow <5% only if site standard 
permits.
 H2S thresholds must be configurable; seed standard reference values such as TLV 1 ppm and IDLH 50 ppm as default 
guidance.
 CO thresholds must be configurable; seed standard reference values such as TLV 25 ppm and IDLH 1200 ppm as default 
guidance.
 Tester identity and gas instrument calibration date must be recorded with every reading.
 Periodic re-test interval must be configurable by permit type/site/area.
 Permit auto-suspends if required gas retest becomes overdue.
 Gas readings must store timestamp, tester, instrument, GPS if available, permit status at time of reading, and pass/fail 
outcome.
 All readings appear in gas test history and trend chart.
6. Conflict Detection
 Area conflict: incompatible permits active in same defined area/zone.
 Equipment conflict: same equipment tag on two simultaneous active permits.
 Hot work + confined space conflict within configurable radius.
 Hot work + line breaking conflict in same unit or hazard area.
 Excavation conflict with buried services or active underground work.
 Radiography conflict with personnel work in exclusion zone.
 Use configurable incompatible permit matrix per site, unit, area, and hazard class.
 Conflict alert must be sent simultaneously to Permit Issuer, Area Authority, Control Room, and affected permit holders.
 Conflict blocks activation unless Area Authority overrides with written justification.
 Override must be permanently audit-logged and visible in permit history.
 Real-time permit map must display active conflicts by area.
7. Mobile Execution + Offline PWA
 Use PWA with service worker and Dexie.js IndexedDB.
 Store active permits locally for offline field access.
 Allow offline gas test entry, isolation confirmation, workforce sign-in/out, photo upload, and permit comments.
 QR code scanning must identify equipment without typing tag number.
 Photo evidence upload from smartphone must store locally until network is available.
 Performing Authority sign-off must work on mobile at job site.
 GPS coordinates must be logged on permit activation and closure.
 Location check must alert if GPS coordinates do not match stated work area coordinates.
 Show sync queue status: Pending Sync, Syncing, Synced, Failed Sync.
 On reconnect, sync offline entries and re-run conflict validation and gas test validity checks.
8. Shift Handover
 Active permit summary auto-populates in digital shift handover report. No manual re-entry.
 Incoming supervisor must acknowledge each active permit before shift handover is complete.
 Permits expiring within 2 hours highlighted in amber with countdown timer.
 Show gas test validity, next retest due, isolation validity, open conflicts, and active risk summary.
 Permit suspension option during handover. Suspended permit requires revalidation before next shift can restart work.
 Handover acknowledgement is an electronic signature with user, timestamp, IP address, and optional GPS/location.
 Handover history tab shows previous shift transfers and acknowledgements.
9. Workforce / Personnel on Permit
 Track all people working under permit.
 Fields: worker name, company, contractor company, trade, role, phone/contact, badge ID, time in, time out, and signature.
 Identify Permit Holder / Performing Authority.
 Track maximum allowed personnel count.

10. Permit Extension Management
 Allow extension before expiry if permissions allow.
 Extension requires reason, new expiry date/time, current gas test validity, isolation validity, and approver if configured.
 Configurable maximum extension count and maximum total permit duration per site and permit type.

11. Permit Closure Verification
 Closure checklist must include: work completed, tools removed, housekeeping completed, personnel accounted for, 
temporary barricades removed, waste removed, equipment safe, area inspected, and de-isolation completed where 
required.
 Closure can require photo evidence if site config requires it.
 Area Authority closure verification required for high risk permits.
 Permit cannot close if required checklist items are incomplete.
 Closure certificate or permit package should include closure checklist and signoff.
12. Permit Templates and Cloning
 Allow saving a permit as template.
 Allow cloning previous permit.
 Support site-specific templates, permit-type templates, and equipment-specific templates.
 Templates must not copy old gas test readings, approvals, signatures, or history.
 Templates can pre-fill work description, risk controls, PPE, isolation requirements, workforce roles, and permit type-specific 
fields.
13. Permit Dashboard and Control Room View
 Dashboard cards: Active Permits, Expiring Within 2 Hours, Suspended, Conflicts, Pending Approval, High Risk, Hot Work 
Today, Confined Space Entries, LOTO Permits.
 Filters: site, unit, area, permit type, status, risk, contractor, date range, issuer, holder.
 Control room map shows active permits by area on plant layout/SVG.
 Permit type colors and risk badges must be visible.
 Clicking a permit on map opens permit detail.
 Show active conflicts and expiring permits prominently.
